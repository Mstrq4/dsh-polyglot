# DSH Polyglot community code of conduct

This is an original, project-specific community policy for DSH Polyglot.
It applies to issues, pull requests, Discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.
The project is Arabic-first and welcomes participation in Arabic or English from
people of every background, language, ability, and experience level.

مرحبًا بالجميع. نختلف حول الأفكار باحترام، ونساعد المساهمين الجدد، ونحمي خصوصية
الآخرين. لا نقبل الإهانة أو التحرش أو التمييز أو نشر المعلومات الخاصة.

## Be constructive

- Critique the code, translation, or idea, not the person.
- Explain terminology and cultural context without mocking language proficiency.
- Give actionable feedback, acknowledge uncertainty, and respect reasonable boundaries.
- Credit others' work and preserve its license and attribution.
- Welcome questions and corrections; do not pressure contributors into unpaid work or immediate replies.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized conduct, deliberate
intimidation, repeated personal attacks, spam, and publishing private information
without consent are not allowed. Do not expose another person's credentials,
conversation contents, screenshots, or private contact details.

## Reporting and moderation

The project maintainer is [Mstrq4](<https://github.com/Mstrq4>). For a conduct concern,
use a private contact method actually listed on that profile, if available; ask
for a private channel without posting the incident's sensitive details publicly.
Do not invent a contact address or use a public issue to disclose private evidence.
For software vulnerabilities, follow [the security policy](<SECURITY.md>) instead.

Include relevant project links and a concise description, with only the personal
information needed to assess the concern. The maintainer aims to handle reports
discreetly, but cannot promise absolute confidentiality or a response deadline.
Information may be shared only as needed for assessment, safety, or legal obligations.

Depending on severity and repetition, moderation may include a request to stop,
a warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project
spaces. Serious safety concerns may warrant immediate restrictions. Participants
may request reconsideration through the same available private contact channel.
Rules apply to maintainers and contributors alike; constructive disagreement and
good-faith criticism are not misconduct.
